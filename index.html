What We Do

Three tracks, one community — pick one up or try them all.

ANIMATE

2D & Motion Graphics

Keyframes, easing, character rigging and animated typography for short pieces and title sequences.

EDIT

Video Editing

Cutting, colour grading and sound design — turning raw footage into something worth watching.

CREATE

Multimedia Production

Storyboarding, photography and mixed-media projects that combine everything we teach.
