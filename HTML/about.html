ABOUT US

A club built by students who like making things.

We started as four people editing videos in a lab after class. Today we're a full community teaching animation, video editing and multimedia production to anyone on campus who wants to learn.

MISSION

Make creative tools approachable

We teach the software and skills behind animation and multimedia production in small, hands-on sessions — so anyone can go from "never opened an editor" to "shipped a project."

VISION

A campus known for its creative work

We want our members' showreels, short films and design projects to be something the whole university points to — and a portfolio members can walk into interviews with.

OUR STORY

From 4 students to a creative community.

What started as a small editing meetup has grown into a club for animation, video production and digital creativity.
