Our mission

Why We Exist

The Animation & Multimedia Club gives students a place to learn the full pipeline of visual storytelling — storyboarding, animation, videography, sound design and editing — regardless of their starting skill level.

We run weekly labs, pair beginners with senior members, and ship real projects every semester so members leave with a reel, not just a certificate.
