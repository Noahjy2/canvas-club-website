ABOUT US

A club built by students who like making things.

We started as five people editing videos in a lab after class. Today we're a full community teaching animation, video editing and multimedia production to anyone on campus who wants to learn.

MISSION

Make creative tools approachable

We teach the software and skills behind animation and multimedia production in small, hands-on sessions — so anyone can go from "never opened an editor" to "shipped a project."

VISION

A campus known for its creative work

We want our members' showreels, short films and design projects to be something the whole university points to — and a portfolio members can walk into interviews with.

Our Story

How the club has grown, year by year.

2020

Founded

Five students started running informal video-editing meetups in a computer lab.

2022

First official workshop series

Recognised as a campus club and ran our first structured Motion Graphics course.

2024

Multimedia track added

Expanded beyond video into storyboarding, photography and mixed-media projects.

2026

180+ active members

Now running weekly workshops across three creative tracks, open to every faculty.

What We Value

OPEN

No experience needed

Every workshop starts from the basics. You don't need a portfolio to join, just curiosity.

HANDS-ON

Learn by making

Every session ends with something you built yourself, not just a demo you watched.

SUPPORTIVE

Feedback, not judgement

Work-in-progress is welcome. We critique to help projects improve, never to discourage.
